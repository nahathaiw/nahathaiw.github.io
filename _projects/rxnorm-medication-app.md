## Overview

A team-based university project: a mobile app that digitizes the information
printed on hospital medication bags.

## What I did

- Coordinate Scrum sprints, backlog management and stand-ups for the team.
- Defined the processing pipeline from photo to normalized medication names.

## How it works

1. OCR and LLM-assisted extraction read the text on a medication bag.
2. Drug names are normalized with RxNorm.
3. Normalized names can then be checked for potential drug-drug interactions.

## Results

Not yet measured. The interaction checks are planned features, not validated clinical functionality.
