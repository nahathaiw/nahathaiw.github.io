## Overview

This project grew out of a personal Pine Script and Python trading-and-backtesting
toolkit that I started around 2020. The goal is one place to explore market signals
next to the research context behind them.

## What I did

- Built an LLM-assisted dashboard with hybrid retrieval-augmented generation and tool calling.
- Added multi-timeframe charting and live company fundamentals.
- Reworked a rules-based stock-analysis workflow into a tool-assisted interface.
- Prototyped strategies, indicators and entry/exit alerts in Pine Script, TradingView and QuantConnect, with historical backtesting.
